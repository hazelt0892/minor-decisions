What is minor decisions?

Minor decisions is here to make life's little dilemmas a little easier. Whether it's a choice you've been putting off or a question that has no business taking this much thought, there's something for everyone. From everyday decisions to the silly, quirky and downright ridiculous, we've got you covered! Explore a collection of decision trees designed to help you weigh up your options and reach a verdict, so you can spend less time overthinking and more time getting on with things. After all, life's too short to spend so long deciding what to eat for dinner.
